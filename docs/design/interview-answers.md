  Light, airy tables with one accent color, stats with deltas, tabs and filter chips.

  A name's page: header card, tabs, and key-value record chips with copy and explorer links.
- **2G Hyperliquid** **[chosen]**: Pro terminal. <https://app.hyperliquid.xyz/trade>

- **2H Aave** **[chosen]**: Money and risk. <https://app.aave.com/reserve-overview/?underlyingAsset=0xc02aaa39b223fe8d0a0e5c4f27ead9083c756cc2&marketName=proto_mainnet_v3>
